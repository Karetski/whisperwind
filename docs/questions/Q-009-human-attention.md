## Question

How are people pulled into runs (questions, approvals, reviews)? How is incoming work rationed so review doesn't fall behind? And what does the product measure about attention?

## Why it matters

This is P-01. Practitioners turn notifications off and engage on their own schedule. Review capacity is the bottleneck ([problem](../research/problem.md) §1A, §1I).

## Options

- **One inbox across runs,** batched so one sitting clears it.
- **Backpressure:** WIP limits that pause new work when unreviewed changes pile up.
- **Pull, not push:** no interruptions by default, with notification rules opt-in.
- **Attention metrics:** review minutes per merged change, time waiting on a person.

## Prior position

Human steps answerable from any process, and a "needs you" inbox first on the TUI's runs screen.

## What would settle it

Scenarios from Q-001, and the owner's own attention pattern.
