## Question

Given Claude Code workflows and agent view, Symphony, Smithers, Archon and Bernstein, where does Whisperwind stand? And does it build on or integrate with terminal multiplexers such as Rex?

## Why it matters

Building what vendors will ship in six months wastes the effort. The claimed category is now occupied ([landscape](../research/landscape.md)).

## Options

Differentiation candidates from the landscape:
- identity-keyed replay;
- human steps that survive a reboot, and one inbox;
- typed "may have run" outcomes;
- failover across vendors;
- safe outputs;
- one cost ledger;
- fork and rewind over VCS.

## Prior position

The moat was the combination: harness-agnostic × durable × human-aware × local-first × terminal-native.

## What would settle it

Answers to Q-001, [Q-006](Q-006-verification.md) and [Q-009](Q-009-human-attention.md). Then test each candidate against P-11 (does it outlive the next model generation?) and against what vendors have announced.
